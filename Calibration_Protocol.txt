================================================================================
DRONE AIRSPEED MONITORING SYSTEM — ABP3 KNOWN-PRESSURE CALIBRATION PROTOCOL
Version: 2.0 (ABP3 I2C migration)
Target sensor: Honeywell ABP3 differential-pressure sensor, I2C 0x6C
Firmware inputs: ABP3 raw and zeroed signed counts; Pa/count is unknown
================================================================================

1. PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This procedure determines the ABP3 pressure transfer function from real,
controlled differential-pressure measurements:

    differential_pressure_Pa = (Pa_per_count * zeroed_ABP3_count) + offset_Pa

It is a pressure-sensor calibration, not an airspeed calibration. Do not
calculate or report airspeed until this result has been reviewed and placed in
the firmware.

The old ABP2/SPI mock datasets and their constants are not valid for this
procedure and must not be used as input.

2. REQUIRED EQUIPMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- ESP32 with the live ABP3 firmware and serial CSV logger.
- A trusted differential-pressure reference, such as a calibrated low-pressure
  manometer or a pressure calibrator. Record its model, range, resolution, and
  accuracy in the manifest notes.
- Leak-free tubing, fittings, and a way to apply pressure only within the
  verified pressure rating of the ABP3, tubing, and reference instrument.

Do not use a fan setting, a guessed syringe volume, or the old ABP2 transfer
function as the pressure reference.

3. PREPARATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Inspect tubing and ports for leaks, moisture, pinches, or blockage.
2. Keep ABP3 SENSE+ and SENSE− at equal pressure before powering/resetting the
   ESP32. The firmware takes a 32-sample startup tare.
3. Confirm serial output is `source=live`, has 19 fields, and contains
   `abp3_pressure_zeroed_counts` and `abp3_status`.
4. Allow the sensors and reference instrument to stabilize as required by the
   reference instrument's instructions.
5. Create a copy of `calibration_manifest_template.csv` for this session.

4. CALIBRATION TRIALS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For each reference-pressure point:

1. Apply the known differential pressure. Record the sign convention in the
   manifest: positive pressure must mean SENSE+ pressure is greater than
   SENSE− pressure.
2. Wait until the reference pressure is steady.
3. Log at least 30 seconds at 10 Hz using `serial_csv_logger.py`.
4. Save using:

       abp3_known_pressure_YYYYMMDD_pplusNNNN_repN.csv

   Use `pminusNNNN` for negative points. `NNNN` is the nominal magnitude in Pa.
   For example (replace `COM5` with the ESP32 port):

       python airspeed_logging\serial_csv_logger.py --port COM5 --output logs\calibration\abp3_known_pressure_YYYYMMDD_pplusNNNN_rep1.csv
5. Add one manifest row with the exact measured pressure in Pa, not merely the
   nominal setting.

Collect a zero-pressure point and at least five distinct non-zero pressure
points across the safe, expected operating range. Repeat each point three
times where practical. Include negative-pressure points only when the
reference setup can apply them safely and accurately.

5. MANIFEST FORMAT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The manifest maps each live CSV to its independently measured pressure:

    filename,known_pressure_pa,reference_instrument,notes

`filename` is relative to the manifest file. `known_pressure_pa` must be a
numeric signed value in Pa. Do not list old ABP2 mock logs or ordinary live
logs that have no known applied pressure.

6. ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
After the live files and manifest are complete, run:

    python airspeed_logging\calibration_analysis.py --manifest logs\calibration\abp3_known_pressure_manifest.csv

The tool rejects mock data and legacy ABP2 CSV headers. It fits the per-trial
mean zeroed count against the reference pressure and writes:

- `abp3_pressure_calibration.json`
- `abp3_pressure_constants.h`

The output reports Pa/count, pressure offset, R², RMSE, trial noise, and the
source files. Review the result before copying the generated header into the
firmware.

7. ACCEPTANCE CHECKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] All analyzed rows use `source=live`.
[ ] Every source CSV has the ABP3 19-column schema.
[ ] The manifest includes a zero point and at least five non-zero pressure
    points, with repeated trials.
[ ] Pressure polarity is documented and agrees with the fitted slope.
[ ] R², RMSE, residuals, and repeatability have been reviewed against the
    reference instrument accuracy and project needs.
[ ] The generated constants are explicitly approved before enabling Pa or
    airspeed fields in firmware.

8. NEXT STAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Only after known-pressure calibration is approved:

1. Convert zeroed ABP3 counts to Pa in firmware.
2. Compute raw and density-compensated airspeed.
3. Perform a separate Pitot/airflow validation against a calibrated anemometer.

================================================================================
