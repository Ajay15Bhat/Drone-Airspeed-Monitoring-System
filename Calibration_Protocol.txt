1. PURPOSE & OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This protocol establishes the standardized procedure for bench-rig calibration
and empirical characterization of the Pitot-static differential pressure airspeed
sensing pipeline prior to UAV flight integration.

The primary objectives are:
  a) Determine the empirical transfer function between density-compensated Pitot
     airspeed (V_comp) and true reference airspeed (V_ref).
  b) Quantify instrument offset, scale factor (gain), linearity, and Pitot
     recovery coefficient (C_p).
  c) Characterize sensor noise floor (standard deviation), repeatability across
     ascending/descending sweeps (hysteresis), and low-speed cut-in threshold.
  d) Measure step-response settling time (t_settle) under sudden airflow changes.
  e) Provide calibrated gain and offset parameters for the firmware and dashboard.


2. REFERENCE INSTRUMENT SPECIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A dedicated reference-speed standard must be used for all calibration trials.
DO NOT treat the bench fan's potentiometer setting, motor RPM, or PWM dial as
true airspeed without direct validation.

Permissible Reference Standards:
  Option A (Primary Benchmark):
    Calibrated digital hot-wire anemometer (e.g., Testo 405i or TSI VelociCalc)
    with traceable calibration certificate.
    - Operating Range: 0.0 to 20.0 m/s
    - Accuracy: ±(0.10 m/s + 5% of measured value)
    - Measurement Resolution: 0.01 m/s

  Option B (Secondary Benchmark):
    Precision vane anemometer (minimum rotor diameter 16 mm, low-friction bearing).
    - Operating Range: 0.5 to 20.0 m/s
    - Accuracy: ±(0.15 m/s + 3% of measured value)
    - Measurement Resolution: 0.05 m/s

  Option C (Aerodynamic Wind-Tunnel Standard):
    Calibrated contraction nozzle or flow-straightened duct with certified static-
    depression calibration curve: V_ref = K_duct * sqrt(delta_p_duct / rho).


3. BENCH-RIG GEOMETRY & SENSOR POSITIONING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Flow Rig Requirements:
  - Test Section: Flow-straightened round or square duct (minimum diameter 120 mm).
  - Straightener: Honeycomb mesh (cell diameter 4–6 mm, length 30–50 mm) positioned
    immediately downstream of the blower/fan to eliminate swirl and vortex shedding.
  - Test Section Uniformity: Reference velocity profile must not vary by more than
    ±2.5% across the central 50 mm core area at 10 m/s.

Probe Alignment Rules:
  - Axial Position: Pitot probe tip placed centrally along the duct centerline,
    150 mm (±10 mm) downstream of the honeycomb flow straightener.
  - Reference Placement: The reference anemometer probe head must be placed
    coplanar with the Pitot probe tip, spaced 35 mm laterally (cross-stream) to
    prevent aerodynamic wake or boundary interference between probes.
  - Angular Alignment:
    * Angle of Attack (pitch, alpha): 0.0° ± 0.5° relative to duct centerline.
    * Angle of Sideslip (yaw, beta):   0.0° ± 0.5° relative to duct centerline.
    * Alignment verification: Use a digital inclinometer and mechanical alignment
      jig against the rig frame prior to tightening fixture clamps.
  - Pneumatic Tubing:
    * Total pressure (impact/tip port) -> ABP2 Port P1 (High Pressure Port).
    * Static reference (side ports)   -> ABP2 Port P2 (Low Pressure Port).
    * Silicone tubing length: equal length for both ports, exactly 150 mm (±5 mm),
      inner diameter 2.0 mm, routed without sharp bends, kinks, or pinch points.


4. TEST SETPOINTS & TRIAL MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Calibration requires at least five (5) distinct airflow setpoints spanning the
usable bench range, tested across three (3) independent repetitions.

Target Airspeed Setpoints:
  - Setpoint 0 (Tare Baseline):     0.0 m/s  (Fan OFF, duct sealed or zero flow)
  - Setpoint 1 (Low-Speed Cut-In):  3.0 m/s  (Differential pressure ~ 5.5 Pa)
  - Setpoint 2 (Low Cruise):        6.0 m/s  (Differential pressure ~ 22.1 Pa)
  - Setpoint 3 (Mid Cruise):        9.0 m/s  (Differential pressure ~ 49.6 Pa)
  - Setpoint 4 (High Cruise):      12.0 m/s  (Differential pressure ~ 88.2 Pa)
  - Setpoint 5 (Full Scale Dash):  15.0 m/s  (Differential pressure ~ 137.8 Pa)

Repetition Scheme (Multi-Trial Sequence):
  To eliminate order bias, detect aerodynamic hysteresis, and evaluate repeatability:
  - Repetition 1 (Ascending Sweep):   SP0 -> SP1 -> SP2 -> SP3 -> SP4 -> SP5
  - Repetition 2 (Descending Sweep):  SP5 -> SP4 -> SP3 -> SP2 -> SP1 -> SP0
  - Repetition 3 (Randomized Sweep):  SP2 -> SP4 -> SP1 -> SP5 -> SP3 -> SP0

Duration per Setpoint Trial:
  - Flow Settling Time: Allow 10.0 seconds after adjusting fan setpoint for flow
    and boundary layers to achieve steady state.
  - Active Data Logging Window: Exactly 45 to 60 seconds of continuous sampling
    at 10 Hz (450 to 600 CSV rows per setpoint run).


5. STEP-BY-STEP CALIBRATION PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Pre-Test Inspection & Setup:
  Step 1: Inspect pneumatic tubing for moisture, dust, cracks, or pinholes.
  Step 2: Verify ESP32 wiring (BME280 on I2C 0x76, MPU on I2C 0x68, ABP2 on SPI).
  Step 3: Power on the ESP32 and host PC logging software. Allow a 15-minute
          thermal warm-up period for all sensor dies to stabilize.
  Step 4: Record ambient environmental baseline:
          - Temperature (temp_C from BME280)
          - Static Barometric Pressure (pressure_hPa from BME280)
          - Relative Humidity (humidity_pct from BME280)

Zero-Flow Tare Routine (Mandatory before every session):
  Step 5: Ensure fan is completely OFF. Gently place a soft foam cap or sleeve
          over the Pitot probe tip and static ports to eliminate draft.
  Step 6: Start serial logging for 30 seconds at Setpoint 0 (V_ref = 0.000 m/s).
  Step 7: Check differential pressure reading:
          - Unfiltered diff_pressure_Pa must read within ±1.5 Pa of zero.
          - If the average offset exceeds ±1.5 Pa, record this value as
            `diff_pressure_tare_Pa` to be subtracted in pre-processing.
  Step 8: Remove the protective cap without straining the pneumatic tubing.

Executing the Calibration Sweep:
  Step 9: Adjust the airflow blower to Setpoint 1 (target ~3.0 m/s).
  Step 10: Observe the reference anemometer display. Once the reading stabilizes
           (variation < ±0.05 m/s over 5 seconds), note the exact steady-state
           reference speed (e.g., 3.04 m/s).
  Step 11: Begin logging via `serial_csv_logger.py` or set the calibration tool
           with the exact reference speed parameter.
  Step 12: Log continuously for 45–60 seconds. Confirm that `reference_speed_mps`
           in the CSV is populated with the measured reference value.
  Step 13: Stop logging and save the CSV file following Section 6 naming.
  Step 14: Repeat Steps 9–13 for all setpoints across Repetitions 1, 2, and 3.

Dynamic Settling Time (Gust / Transient Test):
  Step 15: Run a step-change trial: start logging at Setpoint 2 (6.0 m/s), log for
           20 seconds, command a sudden step to Setpoint 4 (12.0 m/s) within < 1 s,
           and continue logging for 40 seconds.
  Step 16: Repeat the step down (12.0 m/s -> 6.0 m/s).


6. DATA SCHEMA & FILE NAMING CONVENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CSV Schema Contract (19 Columns):
  timestamp_ms,source,temp_C,pressure_hPa,humidity_pct,
  accel_x_g,accel_y_g,accel_z_g,gyro_x_dps,gyro_y_dps,gyro_z_dps,
  diff_pressure_counts,diff_pressure_inH2O,diff_pressure_Pa,
  abp_temperature_C,abp_status,airspeed_raw_mps,
  airspeed_calibrated_mps,reference_speed_mps

Rules for Calibration Runs:
  - Column `reference_speed_mps` MUST be populated with the reference anemometer
    value (format: `%.3f`).
  - Column `source` MUST be `live` when real ABP2 hardware is connected, or
    `mock` if running hardware-in-the-loop simulation.

Standardized Filename Format:
  calib_<YYYYMMDD>_sp<speed_mps>_rep<rep_num>.csv

Examples:
  calib_20260920_sp00_rep1.csv   (0.0 m/s tare run, repetition 1)
  calib_20260920_sp03_rep1.csv   (3.0 m/s run, repetition 1)
  calib_20260920_sp06_rep2.csv   (6.0 m/s run, repetition 2)
  calib_20260920_step_6to12mps.csv (step response transient test)


7. MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & ANALYSIS METRICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Air Density Compensation (Moist Air CIPM / Tetens Formulation):
  Saturation vapor pressure:
    p_sat = 6.1078 * 10^((7.5 * temp_C) / (temp_C + 237.3))   [hPa]
  Vapor partial pressure:
    p_v   = (humidity_pct / 100.0) * p_sat * 100.0             [Pa]
  Dry air partial pressure:
    p_d   = (pressure_hPa * 100.0) - p_v                       [Pa]
  Moist Air Density:
    rho   = (p_d / (R_d * T_K)) + (p_v / (R_v * T_K))          [kg/m^3]
    where:
      R_d = 287.058 J/(kg*K)  (gas constant for dry air)
      R_v = 461.495 J/(kg*K)  (gas constant for water vapor)
      T_K = temp_C + 273.15   (temperature in Kelvin)

Airspeed Derivations:
  Raw Indicated Airspeed (standard sea-level density rho_0 = 1.225 kg/m^3):
    V_raw  = sqrt( 2 * max(diff_pressure_Pa, 0) / 1.225 )

  Density-Compensated Airspeed:
    V_comp = sqrt( 2 * max(diff_pressure_Pa, 0) / rho )

Statistical & Error Metrics (per setpoint run):
  - Mean differential pressure: mean(diff_pressure_Pa)
  - Pressure noise:             std(diff_pressure_Pa)
  - Mean compensated airspeed:  mean(V_comp)
  - Velocity jitter:            std(V_comp)
  - Mean Bias Error:            MBE  = mean(V_comp - V_ref)
  - Mean Absolute Error:        MAE  = mean(|V_comp - V_ref|)
  - Root Mean Square Error:     RMSE = sqrt( mean( (V_comp - V_ref)^2 ) )
  - Relative Error:             e%   = ((mean(V_comp) - V_ref) / V_ref) * 100%
  - Settling Time:              t_settle (time to enter & remain within ±5% V_final)

Correction Fitting Models:
  1. Linear Calibration Model:
       V_calibrated = m * V_comp + c
       Parameters: Slope (m), Offset (c in m/s), R^2 coefficient of determination.
  2. Aerodynamic Recovery Factor (Pitot Coefficient C_p):
       C_p = sqrt( (2 * diff_pressure_Pa) / (rho * V_ref^2) )
       V_calibrated = (1.0 / C_p_mean) * V_comp


8. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA FOR WEEK 2 PASS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [ ] Zero-flow pressure offset |diff_pressure_tare_Pa| < 1.5 Pa after warmup.
  [ ] Calibration linearity R^2 >= 0.990 across the 3.0 to 15.0 m/s range.
  [ ] Root Mean Square Error (RMSE) of calibrated airspeed <= 0.35 m/s across
      all steady-state setpoints.
  [ ] Repeatability hysteresis between ascending and descending runs < 0.25 m/s.
  [ ] Step-response settling time t_settle (to ±5% of setpoint) <= 0.8 seconds.
  [ ] Output parameters saved to `calibration_parameters.json` and
      `calibration_constants.h`.
